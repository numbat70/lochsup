Join Scotlands only SUP RACING CLUB

The club is open to all competent paddlers, but feel free to message call or drop down to find out if it is for you.

We aim to help anyone who wants to improve their paddling speed and efficiency. This is mostly focused on racing at all levels but also helps touring and other disciplines.

Warning: SUP race training is addictive and may consume all your free time 🙂

We train at Lochwinnoch, (Castle Semple Loch), mid week until Winter when we will go onto a more sporadic “Weather Dependant” weekend schedule.

The sessions are suitable for Touring Boards and upwards but come and see us no matter what you have.

Give us a call, message or pop along for a chat if you are interested.

Get Faster & Start Racing

To get better faster:

  • Read about race training and then do it.
  • Our Performance Blog has lots of info on it.
  • Attend a Ready to Race course
  • Enter a Race
  • Turn up and race
  • Have fun 🙂
  • Join a Race Club or Group

Do these in any order (You probably should Enter a race before turning up :-) )
