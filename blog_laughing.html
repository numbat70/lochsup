Laugh in the face of Adversity

Laugh in the face of Adversity?

April 22, 2023

Laughing, humming, singing and indeed anything that vibrates your neck will calm your nerves down.

Here is why 🙂

Physiology

Your nervous system has two sides to it:

1: Sympathetic = Fight or Flight system. This gets you revved up.

2: Parasympathetic: Relaxing & Calming system: This gets your body to relax and calm down.

They both need to balance but during stress the Sympathetic usually takes precedence. This helps you perform “up to a point”, after that the stress starts to be destructive. You need to calm your body down a bit.

Stimulating the Parasympathetic nervous system is the easiest way to do it.

Luckily there are a load of ways you can do this whilst still paddling.

Breathing Exercises

  • Breathe through your nose
  • Box Breathing https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJJazKtH_9I
  • Light Breathing
  • Deep Breathing
  • Slow Breathing
  • 6 breaths per minute
  • Meditation

Cold therapy

  • Dip your face in cold water or Splash it if you are on your board.
  • https://oxygenadvantage.com/science/vagus-nerve-stimulation/

 Self Talk

  • Prepared phrases that you have used to create a calmer mind will also help you here. 
  • You have to train these phrases in training and actively trust them to calm down your mental state

Vibration

  • Laughing 
  • Humming
  • Singing
  • Gargling (may be mistaken for drowning)

Summary

  • Our bodies are wonderful and complicated things.
  • We take our mental state for granted but there are a lot of easy physical tricks that’s we can use to manage our mood.
